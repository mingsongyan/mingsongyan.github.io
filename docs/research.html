Research

My research focuses on the mathematical foundations of efficient and interpretable machine learning. Using tools from functional analysis, optimization, and dynamical systems, I study how mathematical structure can help us understand and improve modern learning models, particularly neural networks and large language models.

My work centers on two closely connected themes:

  • Efficiency: Exploiting sparsity and low-rank structure, understanding optimization mechanisms, and studying the transferability of graph neural models across graph sizes.

    Papers by topic: [Sparse learning] [Low-rank pretraining] [Graph size transfer]

  • Interpretability: Developing function-space perspectives on deep learning and understanding how transformers implement classical learning algorithms through in-context learning.

    Papers by topic: [Transformers and ICL] [Hypothesis spaces]
